Privacy Policy for Idle Host Tycoon

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Idle Host Tycoon ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the App. The App is developed and published by an independent developer, Chris Bean.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account, and does not directly ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. However, the App uses two third-party Google services that collect limited information automatically, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows optional, opt-in rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure these ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • General, approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions), including through Android's Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution) where supported by your device

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional — declining never blocks access to any part of the App's core gameplay.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers one optional one-time purchase ("Ad-Free Operations") processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We never see or store your payment card details — Google Play handles the transaction and shares with us only that the purchase was made, so your entitlement can be unlocked and restored on reinstall.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No name, email, or phone number collected by us directly
  • No precise GPS location
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK is currently integrated

Locally Stored Data

Your game progress (company name, in-game cash, upgrades, settings, etc.) is saved only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any server we control. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently deletes this data.

How Information Is Used

  • To deliver and measure the rewarded video ads described above, via Google AdMob
  • To process and restore the optional Ad-Free Operations purchase, via Google Play Billing
  • To keep the App functioning correctly and to maintain and improve it over time

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob
  • Google Play Terms of Service (governs Play Billing purchases)

Your Ad Choices

You can manage or reset your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized ads, at any time through your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android, or Google's My Ad Center). If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may be shown a consent request before any personalized ads are shown, in accordance with applicable law.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored game data is deleted the moment you uninstall the App or clear its app data. Data held by Google (advertising ID activity, purchase records) is retained and deleted according to Google's own policies, linked above. To request deletion of any data associated with your purchase, you can also contact us directly.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information held about you (for example under the GDPR or CCPA). Because we do not hold a server-side account or profile for you, most of this information is controlled directly through your device and Google account settings described above. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App. Payment information is never handled or stored by us — all purchases are processed directly by Google Play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
